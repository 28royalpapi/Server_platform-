Terms of Service

Server is a calling application in development. This website is used for account setup and phone verification; ordinary calling is intended to happen in an installed app.

Phone verification

You must control the number you register and must not misuse SMS invitations. Verification delivery depends on a functioning SMS gateway, mobile network, and the recipient's carrier.

Internet calling

Server-to-Server and guest browser audio calls require compatible devices, internet access, microphone permission, and connectivity to a media path. A guest web link is not a cellular telephone call, and will not ring a phone whose internet is off.

Availability and safety

Early builds may be interrupted or unavailable. Do not rely on Server for emergency calls. Continue to use your regular mobile/carrier calling option for emergency services until platform support is explicitly confirmed.

Acceptable use

Do not harass, spoof caller identity, spam invitations, or attempt to access calls or accounts that are not yours.

Effective date: 21 October 2026. This draft requires legal review before public launch.
